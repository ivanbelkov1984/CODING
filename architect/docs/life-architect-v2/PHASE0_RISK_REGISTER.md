## Decision rules

- A `critical` risk cannot be silently accepted.
- Any privacy, security, medical/regulatory, or destructive-migration blocker needs explicit owner approval and evidence.
- “CI green” does not close a risk unless the named required evidence is actually covered.
- R02 remains a **plausible risk**, not a confirmed defect, until the synthetic collision test runs.
